1.1 — Persist wizard state to database is the most important fix on the list. Right now if the server restarts while an admin is halfway through /newclient, they get no response and no explanation. With 771 leads and growing activity, this will bite you.

1.2 — TTL pruning for seen_items is already overdue. You have 15,449 own_response rows and 9,853 Reddit rows with no expiry. This will slow down deduplication checks over time.

3.3 — Client self-service dashboard is the biggest strategic gap. Clients currently can't log in and manage anything — they're fully dependent on you for every keyword change or campaign tweak. Until this exists, Gemin-Eye is a managed service, not a SaaS product.

3.5 — Combine alert + response in one Telegram message — this one is already partially true. The report is correct that Facebook/LinkedIn scan results sometimes split across two messages. Easy fix, high daily annoyance for operators.

1.3 & 1.4 — CORS restriction and Telegram webhook signature are both low effort with real security value. No reason not to do them.
Where I'd Adjust the Priority

3.8 — Stripe billing is marked LOW priority in the report, but I'd argue it should move to Phase 2 alongside the self-service dashboard. You already have real businesses on the platform. Every day without billing is revenue left on the table.

3.2 — Chrome extension is marked HIGH priority but is a 1–2 week build. The bookmarklets work fine and the operations manual is already written around them. I'd keep this in Phase 3 and not rush it.

2.9 — Social proof on landing page — the report suggests anonymised projections. I'd skip that until you have real numbers. Fake case studies are a liability if clients or journalists look closely.
One Thing the Report Got Wrong

The web onboarding wizard scrapes the client's website to derive the business type, offering, and audience — it's in replit.md and the codebase. The gap is only in the Telegram /newclient wizard, which doesn't do the scrape yet.
